CP2K: OpenMP threading for neural-network potentials

Fortran/C · OpenMP · HPC

CP2K is a widely used open-source molecular simulation package. I redesigned how its neural-network potential forces are parallelized, and the change is now in the main repository.

  • 42×faster than serial on 76 cores
  • 57×faster than the previous threaded path
  • 0force difference from serial, at any thread count
